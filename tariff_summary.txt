### RTDC Chain of Hotels & Motels

Rajasthan Tourism Development Corporation (RTDC) is a well-established brand name in the tourism industry, operating 25 hotels, 2 midways, and restaurants in the Rajasthan State. During important fairs and festivals, traditional tents are created to give visitors a taste of Rajasthani traditional culture.

Hotels are strategically situated with its pan presence in the State. Midways offer a refreshing break on the important State and National Highways.

Hotels and midways provide travelers an experience of Rajasthan with safety and security. Variety of rooms such as Suite, AC, Non AC, Standard, Huts, and tents provide solutions suited to visitors. Variety of North & South Indian dishes, Continental and Chinese cuisine along with traditional Rajasthani dishes are served. Available facilities are bars, travel counters, shopping arcades, indoor and outdoor games for children, and city sightseeing tours make the stay in RTDC a complete experience. Space also available to organize gala dinner for groups, birthday, anniversary, kitty party, conferences, and other events.

### Palace on Wheels

Though times have changed and the winds have shifted, the Palace on Wheels cruises along in royal style, the kind only the Maharajas of yesteryears could have perpetuated and enjoyed. The exquisite & exclusive Palace on Wheels, one of the ten most luxurious trains of the world, has fourteen deluxe saloons with modern amenities, wall-to-wall carpeting, elegant interior, a well-stocked bar, 2 dining cars, and personalized service. Graced with a truly memorable royal experience, the journey goes through Delhi, Jaipur, Sawai Madhopur, Chittorgarh, Udaipur, Jaisalmer, Jodhpur, Bharatpur, Agra, and back to Delhi.

**Tours Itinerary (7 Nights/8 Days)**  
Departure every Wednesday, between September to April
